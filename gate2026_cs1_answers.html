Worked notes: General Aptitude and reasoning

Q1 · Antonym vocabulary

A protagonist is the main character or leading person. The opposite role is an antagonist, who opposes the protagonist. Therefore, choose B.

Q3 · Knock-out tournament counting

Each game eliminates exactly one player. Starting with 64 players, the tournament must eliminate 63 players before one champion remains. Hence 63 games are required.

Q4 · Constraints and maximum

The maximum total is 70 credits. Fixed credits are 15 core + 20 project = 35. At least 10 specialization credits must be taken. To maximize electives, assign the minimum 10 specialization credits: 70 − 35 − 10 = 25 elective credits.

Q5 · Implication in logic

The statement is teacher-present ⇒ every student stands silently. Its contrapositive is that if some student is not standing silently, the teacher is not present. The original does not imply the converse (all students standing ⇒ teacher present), so C is not necessarily true.

Q6 · Reading comprehension / inference

The passage says combinatorics is sometimes used in physical modelling, not always, so P is false. It explicitly says probability assignments can be used in some such models to compute averages, so Q is true. Answer B.

Q8 · Piecewise / hinge functions

H_K(S)=max(S−K,0) is zero up to K and then rises with slope 1. Compare the graph’s breakpoints and slopes with the candidate differences of hinge functions. The official key is A; verify the exact plotted graph in the linked paper because the diagram is essential.

Q9 · Constraint reasoning

Use the four observations to eliminate impossible rounds: the final-round throws by the silver and bronze medalists are fouls; after round four, Vesely is second and cannot improve; round four is won by Vadlejch. The official key is A (third round).

Q10 · Probability by counting

There are 36 equally likely ordered pairs (first roll, second roll). Count pairs where the second number is a multiple of the first: for first roll 1 there are 6; 2 gives 3; 3 gives 2; 4 gives 1; 5 gives 1; 6 gives 1. Total 14/36 = 7/18, so answer C.
