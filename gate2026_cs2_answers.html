Worked notes: General Aptitude and reasoning

Q1 · Vocabulary / synonyms

Expedite, hasten, and hurry all mean to make something happen faster. Accelerate has the same meaning, so answer A.

Q3 · Binomial probability

Each day is independently cloudy with probability 1/2. Exactly three cloudy days in four can occur in C(4,3)=4 arrangements. Each arrangement has probability (1/2)^4=1/16. Total probability = 4/16 = 1/4, answer A.

Q4 · Profit and loss

Buy 100/50 = 2 shares of A and 80/80 = 1 share of B. Next-day value is 2×55 + 1×70 = ₹180. Initial investment is ₹180, so profit is ₹0, answer A.

Q5 · Logical implication

The sentence is Rain ⇒ Peacocks dance. Its contrapositive is Peacocks do not dance ⇒ it is not raining. Option C is that contrapositive, so C is necessarily true.

Q6 · Analogy

Water relieves thirst; food relieves hunger. Thus P=Thirst and Q=Hunger, answer A.

Q8 · Hamiltonian cycle

A valid trip must form a cycle that visits each vertex exactly once and returns to its start: this is a Hamiltonian cycle. Check the two given graphs for such a cycle; the official key is A. The graph drawing is essential for demonstrating the exact cycle.

Q10 · Probability and prime sums

There are 36 equally likely ordered dice outcomes. Prime sums possible are 2, 3, 5, 7, and 11. Their counts are 1, 2, 4, 6, and 2 respectively, totaling 15. Probability = 15/36 = 5/12, answer C.
